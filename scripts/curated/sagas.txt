# Sagas for the Reference "Watch order" timeline.
#
#   @saga <slug> | <Name> | <tip EN> || <tip ID>
#   @lane <id> | <label EN> || <label ID> [| fork: Title (year)]
#   Title (year) | <lane> | <release YYYY-MM or YYYY> | <in-story label, optional> | <note EN || note ID, optional>
#
# The ORDER OF THE LINES is the story order. Release order comes from the release field.
# A lane with `fork:` is drawn as a branch that leaves the line at that title.
# Titles must match the Surflix library exactly ("Title (year)"); a title that is not in the library is
# drawn as a "Not on Surflix" placeholder so the chronology never has a silent hole.
# Leave the release month empty when unsure. Leave the story label empty when unsure.
# Run: node scripts/build-sagas.js  (writes lib/sagas.json and reports titles missing from the library)

# found and fixed, so do not "restore" the old numbers:
#   1. Phase 1 was on the SUPERSEDED 2018 "First 10 Years" chart (Iron Man 2010, IM2/Hulk/Thor
#      2011, IM3 2012). Marvel's 2023 official timeline book moved Iron Man back to 2008 and
#      everything under it shifted with it.
#   2. Phase 4-5 was one to two years early throughout, and three pairs were in the wrong order:
#      Wakanda Forever before Love and Thunder, Shang-Chi before Far From Home, and Doctor
#      Strange after Homecoming (its story runs into early 2017).
# AUTHORITY: dates through Wakanda Forever are anchored to the 2023 official book. Everything
# after it is past the book's coverage and is the wiki's own reconstruction - every MCU wiki year
# page carries a {{FanTimeline}} banner. The saga tip says so. Titles the wiki has NOT dated
# (Punisher: One Last Kill) get NO year printed; leave them blank rather than guessing.
