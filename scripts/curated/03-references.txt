Cyberpunk: Edgerunners (2022) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | m | The Cyberpunk 2077 world that Edgerunners animates takes its look from Blade Runner's neon and rain. || Dunia Cyberpunk 2077 yang dianimasikan Edgerunners mengambil tampilannya dari neon dan hujan Blade Runner.

The Creator (2023) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | m | The Creator's glowing, lived-in Asian megacities echo Blade Runner's look. || Kota-kota Asia yang berpendar dan terasa dihuni di The Creator mengingatkan pada tampilan Blade Runner.
