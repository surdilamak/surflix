# Referensi sinematik (SUMBER merujuk TARGET). h = terdokumentasi kuat, m = sering dibahas.

# ---------- 2001 dan anak-cucunya ----------
Moon (2009) > 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) | homage | h | Moon gives its lonely lunar base a gentle cousin of HAL 9000: GERTY, a station computer with a smiley face for a face. || Moon memberi pangkalan bulannya sepupu HAL 9000 yang lembut: GERTY, komputer stasiun dengan emotikon sebagai wajah.
Interstellar (2014) > 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) | inspired | h | Christopher Nolan saw 2001 in a cinema as a kid, and Interstellar shares its silent, enormous way of filming space. || Christopher Nolan menonton 2001 di bioskop saat kecil, dan Interstellar punya cara yang sama sunyi dan raksasa dalam memfilmkan luar angkasa.
Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022) > 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) | homage | h | Reviewers read the rock universe in Everything Everywhere All at Once as a reconstruction of 2001's Dawn of Man; the Daniels have also pointed to the picture book Sylvester and the Magic Pebble for the rocks. || Para pengulas membaca semesta batu di Everything Everywhere All at Once sebagai rekonstruksi Dawn of Man dari 2001; Daniels juga menunjuk buku bergambar Sylvester and the Magic Pebble untuk batu-batunya.
Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022) > In the Mood for Love (2000) | homage | h | The universe where Evelyn and Waymond are quiet, longing lovers borrows Wong Kar-wai's red-lit near-miss romance. || Semesta tempat Evelyn dan Waymond jadi sepasang kekasih yang diam-diam rindu meminjam romansa nyaris-bertemu bercahaya merah ala Wong Kar-wai.

# ---------- Kota-kota masa depan ----------
Blade Runner (1982) > Metropolis (1927) | visual | h | Critics compare Blade Runner's towering, stratified city to Metropolis; Ridley Scott has not been documented naming it as a reference. || Kritikus membandingkan kota Blade Runner yang menjulang dan berlapis dengan Metropolis; Ridley Scott belum tercatat menyebutnya sebagai acuan.
Star Wars (1977) > Metropolis (1927) | visual | h | C-3PO is modelled on Maria from Metropolis: the same gleaming gold, the same human shape built in metal. || C-3PO dimodelkan dari Maria di Metropolis: emas yang sama berkilau, bentuk manusia yang sama dari logam.
Metropolis (2001) > Metropolis (1927) | inspired | h | Osamu Tezuka's manga, which this anime adapts, was inspired by promotional imagery from Fritz Lang's film, which Tezuka had not yet seen. || Manga Osamu Tezuka, yang diadaptasi anime ini, terinspirasi oleh materi promosi film Fritz Lang, yang saat itu belum pernah ditonton Tezuka.
Akira (1988) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | m | Akira's art director Toshiharu Mizutani named Blade Runner as a reference for Neo-Tokyo, saying he watched it over and over. || Art director Akira, Toshiharu Mizutani, menyebut Blade Runner sebagai rujukan Neo-Tokyo — ia mengaku menontonnya berulang-ulang.
Ghost in the Shell (2017) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | m | Critics note that the live-action Ghost in the Shell echoes Blade Runner's neon, giant holographic ads and hazy skyline. || Kritikus mencatat Ghost in the Shell live-action menggemakan neon, iklan hologram raksasa, dan cakrawala berkabut Blade Runner.
Batman Begins (2005) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | h | Christopher Nolan has said Blade Runner shaped how Gotham was built and shot in Batman Begins, though he calls it hard to separate conscious homage from analysis of why that film worked. || Christopher Nolan mengatakan Blade Runner membentuk cara Gotham dibangun dan difilmkan di Batman Begins, meski ia menilai sulit memisahkan penghormatan yang disengaja dari analisis mengapa film itu berhasil.
Cyberpunk: Edgerunners (2022) > Akira (1988) | inspired | m | Edgerunners' neon megacity and street kids with heavy tech recall Akira; writer Bartosz Sztybor said he rewatched Akira while writing. || Megakota neon dan anak jalanan bertenaga teknologi berat di Edgerunners mengingatkan pada Akira; penulis Bartosz Sztybor mengaku menonton ulang Akira saat menulis.
Cyberpunk: Edgerunners (2022) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | m | Cyberpunk 2077 staff have named Blade Runner among the works the team studied, though the game's art directors describe Night City's look in terms of their own invented era-styles rather than the film. || Kru Cyberpunk 2077 menyebut Blade Runner di antara karya yang dipelajari tim, meski para art director-nya menggambarkan tampilan Night City lewat gaya zaman ciptaan mereka sendiri, bukan lewat film itu.
The Creator (2023) > Apocalypse Now (1979) | inspired | h | Gareth Edwards has cited Apocalypse Now and other Vietnam War films as inspiration for The Creator, which sets science fiction in Vietnam-style paddy fields; critics describe it as Apocalypse Now with robots. || Gareth Edwards menyebut Apocalypse Now dan film Perang Vietnam lain sebagai inspirasi The Creator, yang menempatkan fiksi ilmiah di sawah bergaya Vietnam; kritikus menyebutnya Apocalypse Now dengan robot.
The Creator (2023) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | m | Gareth Edwards described The Creator as if someone had made Apocalypse Now in the Blade Runner universe. || Gareth Edwards menggambarkan The Creator seolah seseorang membuat Apocalypse Now di dalam semesta Blade Runner.

# ---------- Star Wars dan nenek moyangnya ----------
Star Wars (1977) > Seven Samurai (1954) | inspired | m | George Lucas has credited Kurosawa's films, chiefly The Hidden Fortress; critics see the band-on-a-mission shape of Seven Samurai echoed in Star Wars. || George Lucas mengakui film-film Kurosawa, terutama The Hidden Fortress; kritikus melihat pola sekelompok orang dengan misi dari Seven Samurai bergema di Star Wars.
The Mandalorian (2019) > Seven Samurai (1954) | homage | h | The episode Sanctuary has Mando train a village to fend off raiders, which is Seven Samurai in a different galaxy. || Episode Sanctuary menampilkan Mando melatih satu desa melawan perampok, yaitu Seven Samurai di galaksi lain.
Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) > Star Wars (1977) | inspired | m | James Gunn has said Guardians was intentionally his own version of Star Wars, alongside Raiders of the Lost Ark. || James Gunn menyatakan Guardians sengaja dibuat sebagai versi Star Wars-nya sendiri, berdampingan dengan Raiders of the Lost Ark.

# ---------- Hitchcock ----------
Twelve Monkeys (1995) > Vertigo (1958) | homage | h | Characters in Twelve Monkeys slip into a theatre showing Vertigo, and a wig-and-disguise plot echoes Hitchcock's obsession story. || Tokoh Twelve Monkeys masuk ke bioskop yang memutar Vertigo, dan alur wig serta penyamaran bergema dengan kisah obsesi Hitchcock.
Under the Silver Lake (2018) > Vertigo (1958) | homage | h | Critics read Under the Silver Lake as echoing Vertigo and Rear Window, with a drifter obsessed with a vanished blonde in modern Los Angeles; it is one of many Hollywood nods. || Kritikus membaca Under the Silver Lake sebagai gema Vertigo dan Rear Window, dengan pengembara yang terobsesi pada perempuan pirang yang hilang di Los Angeles modern; ini satu dari banyak rujukan Hollywood.
Under the Silver Lake (2018) > Mulholland Drive (2001) | visual | m | Critics often compare Under the Silver Lake to Mulholland Drive for its sun-bleached Los Angeles mystery of multiplying clues, and the film casts Patrick Fischler, who appeared in Lynch's film. || Kritikus sering membandingkan Under the Silver Lake dengan Mulholland Drive: misteri Los Angeles yang pucat terik dengan petunjuk yang terus berlipat. Film ini juga memakai Patrick Fischler dari film Lynch.
Decision to Leave (2022) > Vertigo (1958) | inspired | h | Park Chan-wook has said Vertigo made him want to be a filmmaker and accepts its influence on Decision to Leave, though he says it worked on him subconsciously. || Park Chan-wook mengatakan Vertigo membuatnya ingin jadi pembuat film dan menerima pengaruhnya pada Decision to Leave, meski ia bilang pengaruh itu bekerja tanpa disadari.
The Silence of the Lambs (1991) > Psycho (1960) | inspired | h | Both Buffalo Bill and Norman Bates draw on the real-life killer Ed Gein; Buffalo Bill is also said to be a composite of other killers. || Buffalo Bill dan Norman Bates sama-sama berpijak pada pembunuh sungguhan Ed Gein; Buffalo Bill juga disebut gabungan dari pembunuh lain.
Pearl (2022) > The Wizard of Oz (1939) | homage | h | Ti West paints Pearl in Technicolor farm-girl daydreams, a twisted answer to The Wizard of Oz. || Ti West melukis Pearl dengan angan-angan gadis petani berwarna Technicolor, jawaban yang kelam untuk The Wizard of Oz.

# ---------- Scorsese dan keluarga ----------
Joker (2019) > Taxi Driver (1976) | inspired | h | Todd Phillips has cited Scorsese's Taxi Driver and The King of Comedy as models for Joker: a lonely, unraveling man in a filthy city. || Todd Phillips menyebut Taxi Driver dan The King of Comedy karya Scorsese sebagai acuan Joker: pria kesepian yang runtuh di kota yang kotor.
First Reformed (2018) > Taxi Driver (1976) | homage | h | Paul Schrader, who wrote Taxi Driver, has said First Reformed revisits its diary-writing loner; the film's structure comes mainly from Bresson's Diary of a Country Priest and Bergman's Winter Light. || Paul Schrader, penulis Taxi Driver, mengatakan First Reformed menengok lagi si penyendiri penulis buku harian itu; strukturnya terutama dari karya Bresson dan Bergman.
The Wolf of Wall Street (2013) > GoodFellas (1990) | visual | h | Scorsese reaches for the GoodFellas toolbox again: a rise and fall narrated by the man himself, a camera that never stands still. || Scorsese kembali memakai perangkat GoodFellas: kisah naik-jatuh yang dituturkan tokohnya sendiri, kamera yang tak pernah diam.
The Sopranos (1999) > GoodFellas (1990) | homage | h | A dozen or more GoodFellas actors turned up on The Sopranos, and the show wears the film's influence openly. || Belasan aktor GoodFellas muncul di The Sopranos, dan serialnya terang-terangan membawa pengaruh film itu.
The Batman (2022) > Chinatown (1974) | inspired | h | Matt Reeves has cited Chinatown as a model for The Batman's noir detective story. || Matt Reeves menyebut Chinatown sebagai model kisah detektif noir di The Batman.
The Batman (2022) > Taxi Driver (1976) | inspired | m | Reeves has talked about Taxi Driver when describing Bruce Wayne as a lonely man writing in a journal. || Reeves pernah membahas Taxi Driver saat menggambarkan Bruce Wayne sebagai pria kesepian yang menulis jurnal.
The Batman (2022) > Se7en (1995) | visual | m | Critics compare The Batman to Se7en for its endless rain, puzzle-leaving killer and gloomy detective mood; Reeves has said the Riddler came from the real Zodiac Killer, not Fincher's film. || Kritikus membandingkan The Batman dengan Se7en karena hujan tak henti, pembunuh bertekateki, dan suasana detektif yang muram; Reeves menyebut Riddler dibentuk dari Zodiac sungguhan, bukan film Fincher.

# ---------- Kubrick, Aronofsky, Refn ----------
Bronson (2009) > A Clockwork Orange (1971) | visual | m | Critics read Bronson as a tribute to A Clockwork Orange: classical music over violence and a narrator who talks straight to camera. Nicolas Winding Refn has cited Kubrick's use of music in it. || Kritikus membaca Bronson sebagai penghormatan untuk A Clockwork Orange: musik klasik di atas kekerasan dan narator yang bicara langsung ke kamera. Nicolas Winding Refn pernah menyebut pemakaian musik Kubrick di film itu.
Trainspotting (1996) > A Clockwork Orange (1971) | inspired | m | Danny Boyle screened A Clockwork Orange for the Trainspotting cast during rehearsals, alongside GoodFellas, The Hustler, The Exorcist and Near Dark. || Danny Boyle memutar A Clockwork Orange untuk para pemeran Trainspotting saat latihan, bersama GoodFellas, The Hustler, The Exorcist, dan Near Dark.
The Substance (2024) > The Shining (1980) | homage | m | The Substance recreates The Shining's red bathroom and patterned corridor carpet, and has Elisabeth pound on a bathroom door the way Jack Torrance did. || The Substance membangun ulang kamar mandi merah dan karpet berpola di koridor panjang The Shining, dan membuat Elisabeth menggedor pintu kamar mandi seperti Jack Torrance.
Requiem for a Dream (2000) > Perfect Blue (1998) | homage | h | Darren Aronofsky tried to buy the remake rights to Perfect Blue (the deal fell through) and recreated its bathtub shot in Requiem for a Dream, which he called an homage. || Darren Aronofsky mencoba membeli hak remake Perfect Blue (kesepakatan batal) dan menciptakan ulang bidikan bak mandinya di Requiem for a Dream, yang ia sebut penghormatan.
Pi (1998) > Eraserhead (1977) | visual | m | Critics compare Pi to Eraserhead for its grainy black-and-white paranoia, but Aronofsky called the comparison "a shallow connection" in a 1998 interview. || Kritikus membandingkan Pi dengan Eraserhead karena paranoia hitam-putihnya yang berbintik, tetapi Aronofsky menyebut perbandingan itu "a shallow connection" dalam wawancara 1998.

# ---------- Tarantino dan sekitarnya ----------
The Hateful Eight (2015) > The Thing (1982) | homage | h | Tarantino screened The Thing for his cast and used unused Ennio Morricone music from it. A snowed-in room full of liars does the rest. || Tarantino memutar The Thing untuk para pemainnya dan memakai musik Ennio Morricone yang tak terpakai darinya. Ruangan terkepung salju penuh pembohong menyelesaikan sisanya.
Scary Movie (2000) > I Know What You Did Last Summer (1997) | homage | h | Scary Movie spoofs I Know What You Did Last Summer, along with Scream. || Scary Movie memarodikan I Know What You Did Last Summer, bersama Scream.
Scary Movie 3 (2003) > Signs (2002) | homage | h | Scary Movie 3 stages its own crop circles and farmhouse siege as a spoof of Signs. || Scary Movie 3 membuat lingkaran tanaman dan pengepungan rumah pertanian sendiri sebagai parodi Signs.
Scary Movie 4 (2006) > The Village (2004) | homage | h | Scary Movie 4 spoofs The Village, plus War of the Worlds and Saw. || Scary Movie 4 memarodikan The Village, ditambah War of the Worlds dan Saw.

# ---------- Wong Kar-wai, Ozu, Bergman ----------
Moonlight (2016) > In the Mood for Love (2000) | visual | h | Barry Jenkins has cited Wong Kar-wai as a guiding influence: saturated colour, longing in close-up, love told in glances. || Barry Jenkins menyebut Wong Kar-wai sebagai pengaruh utama: warna jenuh, kerinduan dalam close-up, cinta yang diceritakan lewat tatapan.
Perfect Days (2023) > Tokyo Story (1953) | homage | h | Wim Wenders is a devoted Ozu fan, and the hero of Perfect Days is named Hirayama, a name Ozu used for characters in several films. || Wim Wenders pengagum berat Ozu, dan tokoh Perfect Days bernama Hirayama, nama yang dipakai Ozu untuk tokoh di beberapa filmnya.
Marriage Story (2019) > Scenes from a Marriage (1974) | inspired | m | Critics compare Marriage Story to Bergman's Scenes from a Marriage, though Baumbach himself only named Persona, as a close-up reference he and his cinematographer studied. || Kritikus membandingkan Marriage Story dengan Scenes from a Marriage karya Bergman, meski Baumbach sendiri hanya menyebut Persona, sebagai rujukan close-up yang ia pelajari bersama sinematografernya.
La La Land (2016) > Singin' in the Rain (1952) | homage | h | Damien Chazelle leans on Singin' in the Rain: tap shoes, a lamp-post romance, and a number about being happy. || Damien Chazelle bersandar pada Singin' in the Rain: sepatu tap, romansa tiang lampu, dan nomor tentang bahagia.

# ---------- Horor modern ----------
Longlegs (2024) > The Silence of the Lambs (1991) | inspired | m | Longlegs is a serial-killer chase with an FBI rookie in the line of The Silence of the Lambs. || Longlegs adalah pengejaran pembunuh berantai dengan agen FBI pemula, sejalan dengan The Silence of the Lambs.
Longlegs (2024) > Se7en (1995) | inspired | m | Osgood Perkins has cited Se7en, alongside The Silence of the Lambs, as a model among 90s serial-killer films, and critics note Longlegs shares its bleak mood. || Osgood Perkins menyebut Se7en, bersama The Silence of the Lambs, sebagai acuan di antara film pembunuh berantai 90-an, dan kritikus mencatat Longlegs berbagi suasana suramnya.
Weapons (2025) > Magnolia (1999) | inspired | m | Zach Cregger has cited Magnolia for its ensemble sprawl and epic scale, saying it gave him permission to make a horror epic. || Zach Cregger menyebut Magnolia karena keluasan ansambel dan skala epiknya, yang menurutnya memberinya izin untuk membuat horor berskala epik.

# ---------- Anime, Ghibli, dan sekitarnya ----------
Children Who Chase Lost Voices (2011) > Castle in the Sky (1986) | visual | m | Critics see Castle in the Sky in the underground world of Children Who Chase Lost Voices; Makoto Shinkai has openly admired Ghibli, but the specific echo is a reviewers' reading. || Kritikus melihat Castle in the Sky di dunia bawah tanah Children Who Chase Lost Voices; Makoto Shinkai terbuka mengagumi Ghibli, tetapi gaung yang spesifik ini bacaan para pengulas.
Children Who Chase Lost Voices (2011) > Princess Mononoke (1997) | visual | m | Critics including J Hurtado read the film's creature designs as close to those in Princess Mononoke, though most reviewers compare the film to Castle in the Sky instead. || Kritikus seperti J Hurtado melihat desain makhluk di film ini sangat dekat dengan Princess Mononoke, meski sebagian besar pengulas justru membandingkannya dengan Castle in the Sky.
Inception (2010) > Paprika (2006) | visual | m | Critics compare Paprika's warping nightmare hallway to Inception's rotating corridor, though Nolan has attributed that effect to Kubrick's 2001 and has never addressed Paprika on the record. || Kritikus membandingkan koridor mimpi buruk Paprika yang melengkung dengan koridor berputar di Inception, meski Nolan mengaitkan efek itu dengan 2001 karya Kubrick dan tidak pernah membahas Paprika secara terbuka.
Psycho-Pass (2012) > Blade Runner (1982) | visual | m | Director Naoyoshi Shiotani has said Psycho-Pass drew on Blade Runner, along with Minority Report, Gattaca and Brazil. || Sutradara Naoyoshi Shiotani menyatakan Psycho-Pass mengambil dari Blade Runner, bersama Minority Report, Gattaca, dan Brazil.

# ---------- Serial yang menyebut guru-gurunya ----------
Mr. Robot (2015) > Fight Club (1999) | inspired | h | Sam Esmail has acknowledged Fight Club as a source: a sleepless narrator whose second self wants to burn down the system. || Sam Esmail mengakui Fight Club sebagai sumber: narator yang tak bisa tidur dan diri keduanya yang ingin membakar sistem.
Black Mirror (2011) > The Twilight Zone (1959) | inspired | h | Charlie Brooker has described Black Mirror as the modern Twilight Zone: self-contained stories where technology plays the monster. || Charlie Brooker menggambarkan Black Mirror sebagai Twilight Zone masa kini: cerita mandiri di mana teknologi menjadi monsternya.
Becoming Jane (2007) > Pride & Prejudice (2005) | inspired | m | Becoming Jane dramatises Jane Austen's youthful romance with Tom Lefroy and presents it as the seed of Pride and Prejudice; the 2005 film is one adaptation of that novel. || Becoming Jane mendramatisasi romansa muda Jane Austen dengan Tom Lefroy dan menampilkannya sebagai benih Pride and Prejudice; film 2005 adalah salah satu adaptasi novel itu.
